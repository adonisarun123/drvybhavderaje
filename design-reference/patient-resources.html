PATIENT RESOURCES

A little preparation. A clearer conversation.

Practical information to help you feel ready for your first consultation.

Before your visit

Confirm the date, time and location. Make a short list of your main concerns and the questions you want to ask.

What to bring

Bring relevant reports, referral letters, previous treatment records and a medication list. Check with the clinic if you are unsure which documents are needed.

Travelling to Bengaluru?

Speak with the clinic before booking travel. Confirm your appointment and ask whether you should plan for any additional consultations.

Questions worth asking

What are my options? What are the limitations and risks? What does recovery involve? What follow-up will I need? What is included in the estimated cost?

Frequently asked questions

How do I arrange a consultation?+

Call the clinic or use the consultation request form. A request is not a confirmed appointment; the clinic will confirm the location, date and time.

Where does Dr Vybhav consult?+

The current practice website lists Aesthetica Veda clinics in Whitefield, HRBR Layout and Koramangala, Bengaluru. Please confirm the location and availability when booking.

I’m travelling from another city. Where should I start?+

Contact the clinic before making travel arrangements. Ask which records to bring and confirm your appointment and clinic location.

What should I bring to my first appointment?+

Bring relevant reports, referral letters, previous treatment records and a list of current medications. Writing down your questions can help you make the most of the visit.
