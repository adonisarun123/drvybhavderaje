How do I arrange a consultation?+

Call the clinic or use the consultation request form. A request is not a confirmed appointment; the clinic will confirm the location, date and time.

Where does Dr Vybhav consult?+

The current practice website lists Aesthetica Veda clinics in Whitefield, HRBR Layout and Koramangala, Bengaluru. Please confirm the location and availability when booking.

I’m travelling from another city. Where should I start?+

Contact the clinic before making travel arrangements. Ask which records to bring and confirm your appointment and clinic location.

What should I bring to my first appointment?+

Bring relevant reports, referral letters, previous treatment records and a list of current medications. Writing down your questions can help you make the most of the visit.
