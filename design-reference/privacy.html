WEBSITE PRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

A simple explanation of this website’s consultation request form.

This is a private redesign preview. Online requests are not enabled. Please use the clinic phone number for appointments.

Consultation enquiries

The form is designed to collect your name, phone number, optional email and preferred clinic so the practice can respond to your request. Do not submit medical reports, photographs or detailed health information here.

Your choice

Contact consent is required to submit an enquiry. You can instead call the clinic directly. To ask about an enquiry or request its removal, contact connect@drvybhavderaje.com.

Before this site opens to patients

The practice must confirm its data handling, access and retention arrangements and approve a complete privacy notice before enabling online requests.
